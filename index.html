1. Information Stored on Your Device

Our games save the following information only on your device so the game functions and remembers your achievements:

  • Game progress, scores, personal bests, unlocked items, and in-game settings (sound, vibration, volume).
  • Whether you purchased Remove Ads, so your ad-free entitlement remains active across sessions.
  • Gameplay statistics such as session length, number of games played, and score proximity to your personal best. Each game uses these locally on your device to balance difficulty and decide when optional reward bonuses are offered.
  • A shared on-device record (via an iOS "App Group" container) of which of our games are installed, enabling each title to show installed states on the More Games screen without external network queries.

This information is permanently removed when you delete the game from your device. We have no remote access to it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games are free to download and supported by advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To serve ads, limit frequency, measure performance, and prevent invalid traffic, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • Device identifiers: The Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA, only if you grant tracking permission) and Identifier for Vendors (IDFV).
  • IP address and approximate location: Derived geographic location at the city or regional level (never precise GPS data).
  • Device and app telemetry: Device model, operating system version, system language, and app version.
  • Ad interaction metrics: Impressions, views, clicks, and completion duration for rewarded video ads.
  • Diagnostics: Performance and crash diagnostics related to ad SDK operation.

For detailed information on how Google manages advertising data, visit:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policies & Ad Settings

Types of Ads in Our Games

  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen break ads between games or rounds. Purchasing Remove Ads permanently disables these ads.
  • Optional Rewarded Videos: Optional video ads you can choose to watch for an in-game booster, revive, or bonus. These are 100% voluntary and remain available even after purchasing Remove Ads.

Your Tracking Choice on iOS (App Tracking Transparency)

Upon initial launch on iOS 14.5+, the system presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asking whether you permit the app to track activity across other companies' apps and websites:

  • If you select Allow, Google may use your IDFA to deliver relevant, personalized ads.
  • If you select Ask App Not to Track, your IDFA is withheld and ads are served without cross-app tracking. You will still receive ads, but they will be contextual rather than personalized.

You can adjust this permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Players in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland

In compliance with European data protection regulations, players located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland are presented with a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) message prior to ad requests. You may accept, reject, or customize ad personalization choices directly, and modify your consent anytime via Settings > Privacy Choices inside the game.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple App Store)

Our games offer an optional Remove Ads in-app purchase. All transactions are securely handled directly by Apple through the App Store. CyberGrid Studios does not receive, process, or store your credit card, billing address, or Apple account credentials. Apple transmits only a cryptographic receipt verifying that your purchase succeeded so the game can remove interstitial ads. Refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for purchase data handling.

4. The More Games Screen

Our games include an in-game More Games screen showcasing our other mobile titles. Tapping Get redirects directly to that title's official App Store product page. We do not provide compensation for installs, nor do we track conversion metrics beyond the on-device App Group record described in Section 1.

5. Support & Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any details you provide. We use this information solely to assist you, troubleshoot technical issues, or respond to inquiries. Support correspondence is deleted once your request is resolved, unless retention is legally required.

8. Data Retention & Children's Privacy

Data Retention

  • On-device data: Retained locally until you delete or uninstall the game from your iOS device.
  • Advertising data: Retained by Google in accordance with Google's data retention standards.
  • Support emails: Purged once user requests are fully resolved.

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Our mobile games are designed for general audiences and are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information via email support, please contact us immediately for deletion.

9. Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you may hold rights to access, rectify, delete, or export personal data, or withdraw consent at any time.

Because we do not store your game progression on remote servers, your rights can be exercised directly:

  • Delete game data: Deleting the game from your iOS device instantly removes all local save files and preferences.
  • Modify ad consent: Manage permissions via iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and in-game via Settings > Privacy Choices.
  • Google advertising data: Visit Google's Privacy Controls.
