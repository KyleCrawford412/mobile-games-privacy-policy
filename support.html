Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?

If you reinstalled the game or switched to a new iPhone or iPad, your purchase can easily be restored:

  1. Open the game on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear icon) or open the No-Ads Store modal.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.
  4. Sign in with the Apple ID you used for the original purchase. Your ad-free status will unlock immediately at no additional charge.

How do I manage ad privacy and tracking?

You can turn personalized ad tracking on or off at any time directly through iOS:

  • Open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Find the game in the list and toggle the switch.
  • If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you can also manage your consent preferences inside each game under Settings > Privacy Choices.

How do I reset my game progress or delete my data?

All progress and scores are stored exclusively on your device. Deleting the app from your iPhone or iPad permanently deletes all saved progress, high scores, and settings.
