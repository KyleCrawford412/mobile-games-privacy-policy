## Summary

- **No account needed.** We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
- **Your game progress stays on your device.** Scores, settings, and progress are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. We do not run our own servers that receive this data.
- **Ads are provided by Google AdMob.** Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below. You control whether ads are personalized.
- **Purchases are handled by Apple.** We never see your payment details.
- **We do not sell your personal information for money.**

## 1. Information stored on your device

Our games save the following information **only on your device** so the game works and remembers your progress:

- Game progress, scores, personal bests, unlocked items, and in-game settings (sound, vibration, volume).
- Whether you bought **Remove Ads**, so the purchase keeps working.
- Gameplay statistics such as session length, number of games played, and how close you are to your best score. Each game uses these on your device to adjust difficulty and to decide when ads or optional reward videos are offered.
- A shared record, kept in an iOS "App Group" container on your device, of which of our games are installed. It lets one of our games know whether to suggest another one on its **More Games** screen. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

This information is removed when you delete the game. We cannot access it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games are free and supported by ads. Ads are served by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

- **Device identifiers:** the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking, see below) and the identifier for vendors (IDFV).
- **IP address** and the **approximate location** derived from it (city or region level, never precise GPS location).
- **Device and app information:** device model, operating system version, language, app version.
- **Ad interaction data:** which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped, and how long video ads were watched.
- **Diagnostics:** crash and performance data related to ad delivery.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's policies:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Advertising Policies and ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com/)

### Types of ads in our games

- **Ads between games:** a full-screen ad may appear between rounds. Buying **Remove Ads** turns these off.
- **Optional reward videos:** you can choose to watch a video to receive an in-game bonus (for example, a revive or a booster). These are always your choice and stay available after buying Remove Ads.

### Your tracking choice on iOS (App Tracking Transparency)

The first time you open one of our games, iOS asks whether you allow the game to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

- If you choose **Allow**, Google may use your Advertising Identifier to show more relevant ads.
- If you choose **Ask App Not to Track**, the Advertising Identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized based on cross-app tracking. You will still see ads, which may be less relevant.

You can change this at any time in **iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**.

### Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message from Google (a Google-certified consent management platform) before any ads are requested. You can:

- accept or reject personalized advertising and other purposes listed in the message; and
- change your choices at any time from **Settings > Privacy choices** inside the game.

If you do not consent to personalized ads, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use limited data (such as approximate location and the content of the app) and are not based on your past behavior.

## 3. In-app purchases (Apple)

Our games offer one optional purchase, **Remove Ads**. Payments are processed entirely by **Apple** through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, card number, or billing address. Apple tells the game only whether the purchase was successful, so it can turn off ads between games. Apple's handling of your data is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 4. Our More Games screen

Each game has a **More Games** screen listing our other games. Tapping **Get** opens that game's page in the Apple App Store. We do not reward you for installing other games and we do not track whether you install them, beyond the on-device record described in section 1.

## 5. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to answer you, and we delete support emails once your request is resolved, unless we need to keep them to meet a legal obligation.

## 7. Sharing

We share information only with:

- **Google (AdMob)**, as described in section 2, to show and measure ads.
- **Apple**, which processes purchases and provides the App Store.
- **Authorities**, if required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws (for example, California), allowing advertising partners to use identifiers for personalized ads can count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out by choosing **Ask App Not to Track** (or turning off **Allow Apps to Request to Track** in iOS Settings), and, where offered, through **Settings > Privacy choices**.

## 8. Data retention

- **On-device data** stays on your device until you delete the game.
- **Advertising data** is retained by Google according to Google's policies.
- **Support emails** are deleted once your request is resolved.

## 9. Children

Our games are not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain uses, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we do not keep your game data on our servers, most of these rights are easiest to exercise directly:

- **Delete your game data:** delete the game from your device.
- **Withdraw or change ad consent:** use **Settings > Privacy choices** in the game (EEA, UK, Switzerland) and **iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**.
- **Data held by Google for ads:** see [Google's privacy controls](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy) and [ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

For any other request, email [kylec412@gmail.com](mailto:kylec412@gmail.com). We will respond within 30 days.

## 11. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on approved safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for these transfers.

## 12. Security

Game data is stored in your device's app storage, which iOS protects. Advertising data sent to Google is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 13. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the App Store release notes.
