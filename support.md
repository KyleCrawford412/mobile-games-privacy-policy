# Support

Thanks for playing! If something isn't working, or you have a question, we're happy to help.

**Email:** [kylec412@gmail.com](mailto:kylec412@gmail.com)
We usually reply within 2 business days.

This page covers all of our games: **Jelly Drop**, **Bento Fit**, **Aegis War**, **Astral Blade**, and **Botanical Zen**.

## Contacting us about a problem

To help us fix it quickly, please include:

- the game's name;
- your device model (for example, iPhone 15) and iOS version (**Settings > General > About**);
- the game version (shown on the game's App Store page under **Version History**);
- what happened, and what you expected to happen;
- a screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

## Purchases

### What does Remove Ads do?

**Remove Ads** is a one-time purchase that turns off the ads shown between games, permanently, in the game where you buy it. Optional reward videos (for example, watching a video to revive) stay available, because they are always your choice.

### I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads between games

1. Open the game and tap the **Remove Ads** button (the crown or ribbon icon at the top of the screen).
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
3. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy it (**Settings > [your name] > Media & Purchases**).

If ads between games still appear after restoring, email us with your device model and the game's name.

### Does Remove Ads carry over to my other devices?

Yes. Install the game on your other iPhone or iPad with the same Apple ID, open **Remove Ads**, and tap **Restore Purchases**.

### Does Remove Ads work in all of your games?

Each game has its own Remove Ads purchase. Buying it in one game removes the ads between games in that game only.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it.

## Ads and privacy

### Why does the game show ads?

Our games are free to play. Ads between games, and optional reward videos, pay for development. You can remove the ads between games with the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

### How do I change my ad privacy choices?

- **iPhone and iPad tracking:** go to **iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking** and turn tracking off or on for the game.
- **EEA, UK, and Switzerland:** open the game, tap the **Settings** gear, then **Privacy choices**, to review or change your consent for personalized ads.

### I saw an inappropriate ad

Please email us the game's name, roughly when you saw it, and the advertiser's name or a screenshot if you have one. We'll block it.

### How do I delete my data?

Your game progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes that data. For data used by Google for advertising, see our [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/). For any other request, email us.

## Game settings

### How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the **Settings** gear at the top of the screen. You can switch **Sound Effects** and **Haptic Vibration** on or off and adjust the volume.

### I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device. It is lost if the game is deleted, or if your device is restored from a backup made before you played. Progress does not transfer between devices.

### The game is slow, freezes, or crashes

1. Close the game completely (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.
2. Make sure your device and the game are up to date.
3. Restart your device.

If it keeps happening, email us with your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

## Game tips

### Jelly Drop

- Drag left or right to aim, then release to drop the gummy.
- Matching gummies merge when they touch: below, left, or right. Merging with two or three neighbors at once gives a bigger gummy.
- The **NEXT** bubble shows the gummy you'll get after this one.
- **Smash** removes one gummy (one free per game). **Rainbow** turns your gummy into a wildcard that merges with anything.

### Bento Fit

- Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Or tap a piece, then tap any square it should cover.
- Fill a whole row or column to clear it. A gold glow shows which lines will clear before you let go.
- When no piece in the tray fits, the game is over, unless you revive.
