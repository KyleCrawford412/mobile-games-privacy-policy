## 📄 Key Principles

- **No User Accounts:** No email, password, social login, or personal identifiers required.
- **On-Device Storage:** Game saves, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device.
- **Privacy-First Ads:** Ads delivered by Google AdMob respecting Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and EEA/UK GDPR Consent Management.
- **Secure Purchases:** All in-app purchases processed through Apple StoreKit; no credit card or payment information is ever accessed or stored by CyberGrid Studios.
